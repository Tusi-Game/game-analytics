# Phase 00 — Implementation Tasks: Shared Foundation + Operational Envelope
**Source spec:** `phases/00-foundation.md`, `phases/00.5-ops-envelope.md`, `phases/ER-full.md` · `../spec.md` (FR/SC) · `phases/README.md` (dep table) · **Design deps:** none upstream — this IS the substrate under 01–11 · **Status:** task-list draft (2026-07-17)

## 0. Scope & dependencies

This phase builds the **shared substrate every other phase stands on** — the parts of the design that are owned by no single story and must exist, correct, before 01–11 can be built or tested. Per `phases/README.md`, 01 depends on "— (foundation)"; every other phase depends transitively on 01 and therefore on this. It delivers: the canonical envelope contract type + wire-version handling (Foundation §1.1); the BullMQ-on-Redis queue wiring and fast-ack door (FR-006, backbone §3); the **9-step canonical worker op-ordering skeleton** (Foundation §3.1) as a shared kernel with typed-kind routing to consumer hooks (steps 1–6 + the routing seam; steps 7/8 story-body additions are owned by 01–06 but wired here); the **Redis key grammar + data-type palette + open-day bucket lifecycle + rehydrate-on-miss + seeded-marker machinery** (§2); the **flusher + M/N/S/L flush-class merge engine** (§3.2/§3.2.1); **skew-correction + slewing-NTP clock discipline + sanity clamp + monotonicity alarm** (§4.2); **day-seal + quarantine** (§4.3); the **two dedup regimes** (§4.1); **provenance §4.5 derivation** from credential class; the **platform logical-day §4.7** floor; the **ER skeleton tables shared by all** — `GAME`, `EXCEPTION_TALLY`, `EVENT_CATALOG`, `EVENT_DAY_COUNT`, plus the operational entities `IDENTITY_EDGE`, `ERASURE_LEDGER` (Foundation §1.2, ER-full); **scale/backpressure/rate-limiting** (00.5 §2–§6); **GDPR erasure (Q7 four-tier + `ERASURE_LEDGER` + job)** and **DSAR-access job** (00.5 §7/§9); **Postgres PITR backup** (FR-028/00.5 §8); **envelope-encryption of reversible secrets + TLS + operator hardening surface + default-deny PII** (FR-029/00.5 §9); and the **`docker-compose` skeleton** bringing the whole stack up (SC-009).

**FR/SC this phase is accountable for:** FR-006 (fast-ack queue-backed door), FR-008a (dedup regimes — mechanism substrate), FR-008b (skew-corrected event-time + seal + quarantine substrate), FR-009 (write-ahead ordering position in the op-order), FR-010 (results-only store split — enforced by the schema shape), FR-011 (Redis AOF/noeviction posture), FR-011a (idempotent absolute-value flush + classes), FR-027 (config knobs incl. set-once `reporting_offset`), FR-028 (Postgres PITR backup), FR-029 (out-of-DB secret encryption + TLS + operator hardening), FR-030 (GDPR Art. 15/20 access), SC-007 (results-only storage), SC-008 (Redis-loss superset guarantee), SC-009 (single `docker-compose up`). Money-truth (FR-021), retention (FR-015–017), economy (FR-012–014), monetization (FR-018–020) belong to their story phases and are only *wired* here.

### 1.7 Canonical worker op-ordering kernel (Foundation §3.1 — highest rigor)

*The 9-step per-event backbone. Steps 1–6 are the shared front-door realized in 01's worker; steps 7/8 are story-body additions dispatched via the routed-record seam. This kernel defines the ordering + hooks; story phases fill their step-7/8 bodies.*

- [ ] **T-00.33** Implement the per-event pipeline scaffold that runs steps in the **normative order** and exposes typed hooks at steps 7/8 — enforcing the invariant chain **raw-append ≺ seal-check ≺ dedup ≺ durable-immediate ≺ hot-counter ≺ ack** for every kind — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 (op-ordering), §3.3, 01 Design ("enforces the backbone order for everyone").
- [ ] **T-00.34** Step 1 — **Auth-scope + parse**: `game_id` from SDK-key auth; unparseable body / empty `name` → **drop-and-tally** (`EXCEPTION_TALLY`), **not** raw-appended — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 1, §4.4.
- [ ] **T-00.35** Step 2 — **Skew-correct**: compute corrected event-time and corrected **logical** day; future-clamp; 60 s dead-band; unusable client times → `server_received_time` bucket + `time_fallback` tally (event still accepted) — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 2, §4.2, §4.7.
- [ ] **T-00.36** Step 3 — **Kind-route + validate**: `generic` permissive (catalog caps); typed kinds strict payload validation (invalid → quarantine-mark + step 4 then stop); unrecognized `kind` → quarantine-mark `unknown_kind` + step 4 then stop (never coerce to generic) — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 3, §1.1 (open enum), §4.4.
- [ ] **T-00.37** Step 4 — **Write-ahead raw append** (if cold storage on): append the full envelope — accepted *or* quarantine-marked — to the game's corrected-day file, **fsync'd, before any counter/spine/Redis write**; duplicates are appended (dedup is next). Counter-first ordering is a bug — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 4, FR-009, SC-008, §E. *(byte format/lifecycle owned by 07 via bridge 01.5 — see cross-phase deps)*
- [ ] **T-00.38** Step 5 — **Seal check**: corrected logical day already sealed → append went to quarantine tail → tally `sealed_late` → **stop** — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 5, §4.3.
- [ ] **T-00.39** Step 6 — **Dedup gate** (two regimes, never mixed): non-money — `event_id` 24 h marker claimed **here, before any counter/spine/hot write** (raw-append + dedup-claim form one recovery unit), duplicate → stop; purchase — durable `transaction_id` insert-if-absent (05-owned write, 01-owned sequencing), conflict → stop — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 6 (crash-window ordering, ST4), §4.1.
- [ ] **T-00.40** Verify the **crash-safety direction** of step 6 ordering: a stalled-job re-run that died after step 4 but before claiming the marker re-appends (harmless, rebuild re-dedups) and re-claims; a re-run that died after claiming stops on the marker → worst case an **undercount**, never a double-count; purchases immune via the durable gate — encode as an ordering test — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 6.
- [ ] **T-00.41** Step 7 — **Durable-immediate hook** (idempotent absolutes straight to Postgres, never flush-mediated): expose the seam where 02 seeds `first_seen` (session events, sequence A) + sets the bitmap bit (session-start), 05/06 write money (05.5 atomic unit), 05 writes the `PURCHASE_IDEMPOTENCY` row; the kernel guarantees `first_seen` precedes the bitmap write within the same event — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 7, §5, bridge 02.5, bridge 05.5. *(bodies owned by 02/04/05/06; kernel wires the ordering)*
- [ ] **T-00.42** Step 8 — **Hot-updates hook**: expose the rehydrate-on-miss'd open-day-bucket increment seam consumed by every domain owner (01 `cat`/`cnt`, then routed story's accumulators) — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 8, §2.3.
- [ ] **T-00.43** Step 9 — **Ack** the queue job after the pipeline completes — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 step 9.
- [ ] **T-00.44** Define the **routed record** produced once by the front-door and handed to typed consumers: full envelope + resolved kind + stamped `v` + corrected event-time/day + front-door verdicts (dedup-passed, seal-state); single producer (01), three typed consumers (02/03/05); consumers never re-derive a front-door decision — *cite:* Foundation §3.1 (the routed record), §8.7.

### 1.8 The flusher + M/N/S/L flush-class merge engine (Foundation §3.2 / §3.2.1 — highest rigor)

- [ ] **T-00.45** Implement the **flusher** as a BullMQ repeatable job on `flush_interval` (default 5 min) that sweeps the per-domain dirty-registries and upserts absolute values; a retried/duplicated flush must be a **no-op by construction** under every class — *cite:* Foundation §3.2, FR-011a.
- [ ] **T-00.46** Encode the rule **"deltas never flush; only absolutes do"** — anything not expressible as an absolute cell must be a durable-immediate (step 7) write instead — as a design/lint guard on new flushed structures — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.
- [ ] **T-00.47** Implement **class M (monotonic-additive)** merge: `SET value = GREATEST(target.value, EXCLUDED.value)`, `HSETNX` seed, no atomic read required (torn-low read self-heals) — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 class M.
- [ ] **T-00.48** Implement **class N (non-monotonic / mutable-down)** merge: per-bucket monotonic `gen` (small integer column on class-N result rows), seeded from the durable row's `gen` on rehydrate and `INCR`'d inside every atomic block that can move a cell down (MOVE, FX-recompute); the flush reads all cells **and** `gen` in one **Lua `EVAL`** snapshot (uninterruptible); upsert tagged with the snapshot `gen`, rejected `WHERE EXCLUDED.gen ≥ target.gen` — `GREATEST` **forbidden** here — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 class N. *(bucket-key hash-tag `{game_id:mon:day}` noted as future Redis-Cluster concern — flagged §4)*
- [ ] **T-00.49** Implement **class S (set-membership)** merge: `members = target.members ∪ EXCLUDED.members` (set-union, never blind replace), `SADD` seed; HLL sketches under the scale lever merge via `PFMERGE` identically — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 class S.
- [ ] **T-00.50** Verify **class L (LWW-guarded)** path is unchanged: day-less per-user snapshot guarded by `as_of` (`SET … WHERE EXCLUDED.as_of ≥ target.as_of`), per-entry rehydrate — the `as_of` guard is a generation gate by another name — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 class L (verified unchanged). *(structure owned by 03; kernel confirms the guard shape)*
- [ ] **T-00.51** Implement the **mixed per-field merge for `EVENT_CATALOG`** (`cat`, day-less): `count`/`last_seen` by `GREATEST`, `first_seen` by **`LEAST`** (an earlier first-seen must lower it), `property_type_sets` by **union** — a single class label does not fit — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 (mixed structure), 01 Design.
- [ ] **T-00.52** Encode the **class-assignment gate**: every flushed structure declares its class (M/N/S/L or per-field); adding a flushed structure requires stating its class — as a contribution/CI check — *cite:* Foundation §3.2.1 (fixed here; a story never re-chooses its class).
- [ ] **T-00.53** Implement the **seal-time final flush** that finalizes a day's cells, after which the read model stops consulting Redis for that day — *cite:* Foundation §3.2, §2.3, §3.3.
- [ ] **T-00.54** Implement the **read-model live-vs-historical merge** applied once uniformly: sealed days from Postgres only; open days live from Redis buckets, falling back to last-flushed Postgres on missing bucket (post-crash) — *cite:* Foundation §3.3. *(story read-models state WHAT they read, not a new merge rule)*

### 1.9 Skew / clock-discipline / seal / quarantine / dedup machinery (Foundation §4)

- [ ] **T-00.55** Implement the skew-correction formula: `skew = server_received_time − client_sent_time`; `corrected = client_event_time + skew` (only if `|skew| > 60 s`); `corrected = min(corrected, server_now)` (future-clamp); `bucket = logical_day(corrected)`; `corrected` stored as UTC epoch — *cite:* Foundation §4.2, FR-008b.
- [ ] **T-00.56** Implement the **sanity clamp**: an event whose corrected time moves > `clock_sanity_max_hours` (default 26 h) from `server_received_time` is bucketed on `server_received_time` + `time_fallback` tally rather than trusted — *cite:* Foundation §4.2 (guard 1).
- [ ] **T-00.57** Implement the **monotonicity alarm**: a worker observing `server_received_time` move backward between consecutive batches raises an operational alert (the stepped-clock failure this guards) — *cite:* Foundation §4.2 (guard 2).
- [ ] **T-00.58** Implement day-seal + quarantine substrate: a logical day mutable until `D_end + 48 h` then seals; sealed-day / typed-invalid events append to the raw quarantine tail with marker + tally, **never folded** into any aggregate/spine bit/catalog count — *cite:* Foundation §4.3, §2.3.
- [ ] **T-00.59** Implement the **drop-vs-quarantine** decision helper: drop-and-tally (not raw-appended) = unparseable/empty-name/cap-exceeded; quarantine (raw-appended + marker + tally) = typed-invalid/sealed-late/`unknown_kind` — *cite:* Foundation §4.4.
- [ ] **T-00.60** Implement the two dedup regimes as distinct mechanisms: **windowed** (`{game_id}:dedup:{event_id}` atomic check-and-set, TTL 24 h) for generic/economy/session; **durable** (`PURCHASE_IDEMPOTENCY.transaction_id` UNIQUE insert-if-absent) for purchases — never mixed — *cite:* Foundation §4.1, FR-008a. *(the durable table itself is owned by 05; the windowed marker is front-door/01)*

### 1.11 Scale envelope, backpressure, rate limiting (00.5 §2–§6)

- [ ] **T-00.66** Encode the Redis memory budget as configured limits + monitoring: `maxmemory ≈ 3.8 GB`, door watermark at **80 % (~3 GB)**, dedup-marker/open-bucket/membership/staging/queue-backlog line items from the §3 arithmetic — *cite:* 00.5 §2–§3.
- [ ] **T-00.67** Implement the **fast-ack-degrades-to-fast-refusal** backpressure: past the used-memory watermark (80 %) or queue depth ~200 k jobs, the ingest API returns **503 + Retry-After before acking** — never shed after ack; if Redis fully down → 503 everything — *cite:* 00.5 §4, FR-006.
- [ ] **T-00.68** Implement the **Postgres-down posture** as latency-not-loss: durable-immediate writes fail → BullMQ retries with backoff (money exactly-once via durable gate; non-money bounded undercount already accepted); the flush stalls harmlessly and catches up idempotently; day-seal defers on a failed final flush rather than finalizing short — *cite:* 00.5 §4, Foundation §3.2/§6.

## 2. Cross-phase dependencies

Ownership-matrix (Foundation §5) note — this phase **defines the substrate and the shared/skeleton tables**; the story phases **own** the result/spine tables that ride it. Who WRITES vs READS each shared structure:

- **Credential entities (`GAME_SDK_KEY`, `GAME_SERVER_CREDENTIAL`) and their issuance/rotation are owned by phase 10.** The provenance-derivation task (T-00.61) **reads** the authenticating credential's class; it cannot be fully exercised until phase 10 defines the credential registry. Foundation §4.5 is the contract; phase 10 realizes the entities.
- **Raw day-file byte format + lifecycle (open/seal/upload/delete) are owned by phase 07 via bridge 01.5.** The op-order step-4 task (T-00.37) **executes** the append (write position, fsync grain, quarantine-marker vocabulary = the `EXCEPTION_TALLY` reason names); it must not drift from 01.5. The manual-rebuild + ledger-refilter contract (T-00.75) is normative in 01.5 §5 / 00.5 §7.3 — the rebuild *tool* is a later build.
- **Step-7 durable-immediate bodies are owned by 02/04/05/06.** The kernel (T-00.41) only wires the seam + ordering guarantee (`first_seen` ≺ bitmap); `first_seen`/bitmap writes are 02's session path on 04's behalf (bridge 02.5); money writes are the 05.5 atomic unit; `PURCHASE_IDEMPOTENCY` is 05's. The **durable `transaction_id` gate** referenced in step 6 (T-00.39) is 05's write — this phase owns only its *sequencing* (gate before any spine/hot write).
- **Step-8 hot-bucket bodies are owned by each domain owner** (01 `cat`/`cnt`, 02 `sess`/`act`, 03 `eco`/`bal`, 04 `ret`, 05 `mon`/`payer`/`rev`/`stage`). The kernel (T-00.42) provides the rehydrate-on-miss'd increment seam; the domain owner supplies the accumulator logic and its flush class (T-00.52 gate).
- **`EVENT_CATALOG` / `EVENT_DAY_COUNT` skeleton tables (T-00.15) are refined and owned by 01.** This phase declares the shared shape; 01 adds `status` and the per-field flush rules (T-00.51).
- **Class-N `gen` column lives on 05's `MONETIZATION_CELL` / `PAYER_DAY`** — the flush engine (T-00.48) needs those columns; coordinate the column addition with phase 05.
- **Class-L path validation (T-00.50) depends on 03's `BALANCE_SNAPSHOT`** `as_of` guard — the kernel confirms the guard shape; 03 owns the structure.
- **Operator-account hardening entities (T-00.88) are owned by phase 10** — this phase declares the FR-029 requirement; phase 10 builds `OPERATOR_ACCOUNT` + the login/MFA surface.
- **The erasure job (T-00.72–77) write-deletes structures owned by 02/03/04/05/06** — it is the second cross-story write delegation (after bridge 02.5); it executes under 00.5's rules, and its request-verification surface is hosted by phase 10.

## 4. Open considerations / flags for /plan

1. **Class-N Lua `EVAL` + Redis-Cluster hash-tag (Foundation §3.2.1 schema/deploy note).** The `gen` column on `MONETIZATION_CELL` / `PAYER_DAY` and the single-`EVAL` snapshot are single-node-Redis-safe today; if Redis is ever sharded to a Cluster, the `mon`/`rev` bucket key needs a hash-tag (`{game_id:mon:day}`) so all cells co-locate in one keyslot. Flagged by the design as below the no-DDL altitude — a /plan decision on the exact Lua script + key layout. **This is the single hardest piece of the flush engine — build and test class N before any class-N story (05) lands.**
2. **Kernel/story seam boundary.** The op-order kernel (T-00.33–44) is built here but its step-7/8 *bodies* live in 01–06. There is a real sequencing hazard: the kernel must land with stub hooks and a stable routed-record contract **before** 01–06 can implement against it, yet its correctness (ordering, crash-safety) can only be fully tested once at least 01 (front-door steps 1–6) is real. Recommend: build the kernel + step 1–6 realization (01) as one tightly-coupled unit, then open the step-7/8 hooks to 02/03/04/05/06. Foundation says steps 1–6 execute "in the 01 front-door" — the kernel and 01 are nearly the same buildable unit.
3. **Provenance depends on phase 10's credential registry.** T-00.61/62 cannot be end-to-end tested until phase 10 defines `GAME_SDK_KEY` / `GAME_SERVER_CREDENTIAL`. For the substrate build, stub the credential-class lookup behind the derivation interface so the op-order is testable before phase 10 lands.
4. **`reporting_offset` set-once enforcement mechanism (Foundation §4.7).** The design mandates set-once-at-install but the *mechanism* (a config-immutability guard once any data exists) is below the design altitude — a /plan decision. Getting this wrong silently re-buckets history and violates the seal invariant; it deserves an explicit guard + test, not a convention.
5. **PII scrubber placement + performance (00.5 §9).** The default-deny denylist + value-pattern scrubber (T-00.86) runs on the hot path **before** the raw-append (step 3/pre-step-4). Regex scanning every prop of every event at 600 ev/s design peak is a hot-path cost that needs a performance budget; the exact regex set and short-circuit strategy are /plan territory. Coordinate the exact placement with 01's step-3 realization.
6. **Erasure cross-store write delegation (00.5 §7.5, §10).** The erasure job write-deletes structures owned by 02/03/04/05/06 — the system's second cross-story write delegation. The design ratifies this, but the transactional boundary (all-or-nothing across many owner tables, ordering `USER_SPINE` last) and the `awaiting_seal` re-evaluation loop are implementation-level and error-prone; recommend building the job only after all owner tables exist.
7. **Redis SPOF vs SC-009's "modest VPS" (00.5 §8).** Single un-replicated Redis is a 100 % ingest-outage availability ceiling on any Redis crash/OOM/reboot. Accepted for v1, but the operator-facing RTO (AOF-replay minutes) and the optional HA lever should be surfaced in ops docs at build time, not discovered in an incident.
8. **BullMQ `maxStalledCount` + job-id-on-raw-append interaction (Foundation §3.1 step 6).** The stalled-job re-run safety argument depends on every raw append carrying its batch `job_id` for rebuild-time collapse (T-00.32). The exact BullMQ retry/stall config and the job-id threading through the raw append are /plan-level and must be validated against the crash-window argument, not assumed.
